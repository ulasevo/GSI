# GSI interaction and catalogue audit — 16 September 2026

This document records the confirmed baseline before the next substantial work. It is a working reference, not a replacement for the user’s writing or the existing design backlog.

## Confirmed findings

- Entry section cards can paint beyond their grid allocation when their content heights differ, causing rows to overlap on desktop and mobile.
- Homepage navigation preserves `filter` and `view` unevenly; the selected `format` is not consistently retained after refresh or when moving into a generated album room.
- The generated P53 and artist/album rooms do not yet share one explicit navigation-state contract.
- `--site-only` still permits missing P53-cover downloads. That defeats the stated purpose of the flag and adds avoidable network work.
- Radio P53 is now structurally a continuous field rather than a carousel. Its next pass should improve information hierarchy and density without returning to scroll-lock mechanics.
- The P53 landing page currently loses its intended `main`/`nav` containment in the compact stylesheet: the top path falls back to browser-default styling and the page hugs the left edge. Treat this as a contained layout defect for the next P53 pass, not as a reason to alter the transmission data model.

## Accepted catalogue decisions

### Album format inside a filter

An album appears in **Albums** format when:

1. GSI already has a generated album page for it (currently, two or more represented signals); and
2. at least one of its represented songs belongs to the active filter.

The album card represents the whole album page, not merely the matching songs. Its signal count is therefore the total represented count for that album. A future author-written “why in Dreamy” (or equivalent) note may explain the match; GSI must not invent one.

### Markings

Labels must communicate a real state, identity, quantity, or action. They are not ambient decoration. In particular, do not add generic technical-sounding P53 labels such as “current entry leads” unless the phrase tells a visitor something useful that no other element conveys.

## Local progress — 17 September 2026

- The homepage card geometry, shared query-state handling, and filtered-Album rule
  are now implemented in the local build. The final device sweep remains a release
  check, not a reason to reopen their data model.
- Radio P53 now uses contained landing-page styles and native scrolling. Permanent
  transmissions keep their distinct reading composition; their short notes no
  longer stretch to match the P53 explanation, and SHARE gives visible feedback
  when copying is unavailable.
- The entry index is a compact horizontal section strip above real prose rather
  than a sticky desktop sidebar. It remains absent when an entry has fewer than
  three populated sections.
- Artist rooms and entry-page artist links now use the same eligibility helper.
  P53-only records appear honestly as transmissions where their artist qualifies.
- P53 landing and transmission styles now live in dedicated `web/styles/`
  stylesheets.
  This is a contained extraction, not a project-wide CSS rewrite.
- P53 landing query-state and focus behavior now live in
  `web/scripts/p53-landing.js`,
  leaving generated landing markup free of inline behavior while preserving
  native scrolling and the existing focus contract.
- Permanent transmission route and share behavior now live in
  `web/scripts/p53-transmission.js`; the generated context node carries only the
  page-specific values needed by that helper.
- P53 language now uses `CURRENT TRANSMISSION` for the live slot and
  `PAST TRANSMISSION` for historical slots across the landing page, permanent
  transmission pages, and the homepage broadcast label. Historical cards are
  intentionally compact on mobile as well as desktop.
- Homepage presentation is authored in ordered layers under `web/styles/home/`
  and assembled into `site/styles/gsi-home.css`; state remains in
  `web/scripts/home-page.js`; filter data is emitted as a small JSON script node so the
  behavior file stays static without disturbing query state.
- Artist and album room navigation now use external `web/scripts/artist-room.js`
  and `web/scripts/album-room.js` helpers with JSON data nodes, reducing duplicated inline
  query-state logic without changing their visual structure.
- Artist-room album titles now link to generated album rooms, and album-room
  artist labels are links whenever the artist room exists. Both paths retain
  the visitor's filter/view/format context.
- Provider links are normalized at source-read time, fallback searches include
  album context, and `site/data/catalog.json` records canonical/search status plus
  local cover dimensions and validity for every generated signal.
- Artist rooms now have a deliberately opt-in local-image contract. The generated
  `site/data/artists.json` manifest records artist-room eligibility, represented
  albums, and whether a reviewed local artist image exists; absent art remains an
  explicit fallback rather than an invented or scraped image.
- When artist art is present, the artist room uses one shared hero field for the
  image, name, count, and note. The image itself has no control-like border or
  action treatment; the surrounding field carries the GSI geometry.
- The P53 landing description now has a restrained accent spine and gradient
  anchor, while its large current-card watermark is kept as background texture
  behind the copy rather than a competing control.
- `python build.py --site-only --validate-links` now checks generated internal
  pages, copied assets, fragments, and stable JavaScript route attributes without
  making network requests or probing Spotify/Apple Music URLs.
- A representative headless sweep at 390, 768, and 1440px reports no horizontal
  overflow and no page errors on the homepage, P53 landing, Metric artist room,
  or a permanent P53 transmission. This is a confidence check, not a substitute
  for the final Pixel/tablet pass.
- A full generated-page sweep (53 content pages plus the generated 404 page; 54
  HTML files total) at 390px and 1440px now reports no horizontal overflow or
  page errors. The no-filter homepage control row needed
  an explicit `box-sizing`/width containment guard; it is now covered by the
  same responsive rule as filtered states.
- Source preflight now runs before `build_entries()`, blocking malformed columns,
  unknown tags, route collisions, unsafe asset paths, invalid P53 current
  membership, and missing site-only entry sources. The focused offline test
  suite covers those blockers, metadata-sync prose preservation/idempotence,
  route inventory, and managed stale-page reconciliation.
- Generation now derives entry, P53, artist, and qualifying-album relationships
  from one prepared inventory. The generated `site/data/generation.json` manifest
  makes those routes and expected page families inspectable without loading review
  prose. Entry, artist, and album stale HTML is removed only inside its managed
  generated directory; historical P53 slug pages are retained as permanent links,
  while replaceable landing aliases are reconciled.
- The generation manifest is now part of link validation. P53 landing state uses the
  shared filter/view/format sanitizer, and album-room song links preserve an incoming
  artist context instead of dropping it between album and entry pages.
- The post-1.0 migration has crossed the source, page, and manifest boundaries
  without changing generated routes or authored prose: source preparation is in
  `builder/source_pipeline.py`, Radio P53 pages in `builder/p53_pages.py`, and
  artist/album rooms in `builder/catalog_pages.py`. The generated route and media
  manifests now live in `builder/manifests.py`; entry reading-room parsing and
  generation now live in `builder/entry_pages.py`, homepage rendering in
  `builder/home_page.py`, and 404 rendering in `builder/error_pages.py`. The
  root `build.py` remains a compatibility facade and `builder/legacy_pipeline.py`
  now contains orchestration plus compatibility exports only, with explicit
  source/config/output paths where renderers need them.

Still not treated as complete: an actual final-device / deployed-site sweep, a
deliberate image budget, and any public submission or field-guide feature.
