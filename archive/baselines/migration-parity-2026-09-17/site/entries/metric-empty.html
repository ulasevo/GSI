Charge

The force, momentum or emotional voltage the song carries.

Strength. Makes me feel aware of my current state.

Sonical Attraction

The sounds, textures and production details that pull me back.

The distinct inverted V shape in the rhythm of the song , mixed with the blandness in change of lyrical emotion.. Perfectly matches the depicted inability to see what was coming but being met with damage and having to give in once it’s all too late. Even the starting riff’s excitement is met with the riff at the very end’s decline. The distortion kicking in, in silence and after harmony of the initial lyrical part is almost like a strong bass drop in a sense.

Version of ulaş

The version of me this song holds, recalls or calls forward.

I discovered this entire album in between the midterm 2 grind. The first few listens were each incrementally pleasant, and currently it's one of my favorites off the album. Though that doesn’t really mean much because I’ll be saying that for at least 8 other songs in this album.

Comment

Anything that refuses a cleaner category.

The song begins in some form of suspicion from what I can tell. Initially it's just recognizing the damaging system, then trying to precisely figure out what it was “Ask the line on your face, what the line on your hand meant” perfectly matches that, but then it's more centered on the feeling of the current and then the crash. Quite difficult to describe, but personally I find that there is power in being able to go over something. “Sickness was fixing me some”, it always does. Hardships, breakups have always been things that immensely improve who I am, yet the very next line “coughed out my heart at the last stop” and the ending line are all perfect depictions of the price they make you pay. Still, something is over, and the feeling of it is over as well. It’s empty.
